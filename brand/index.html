Brand brief

What DAD looks and sounds like, so every label, post, sign and page comes out as one brand. When something new is designed, it starts here and the inspiration board.

Working brief. Colors and type are what the site uses today. Logo concepts are on the logo identity canvas; nothing is final until the LLC name check clears.

Who we are

The one-line version, and what sits under it.

What we make

Lathe-cut 7" and 10" records, cut one at a time in Athens, Georgia.

For whom

Athens bands and artists who want 10 to 50 real records for a release, a tour, a gift or the merch table.

Why us

Short runs, local pickup, a person you can call, and records made by hand rather than at a plant.

Archetypes

Three characters, in this proportion. The first leads every decision; the other two keep it from going stiff or precious.

Logo

DADRC wordmark: DAD in the text color, RC always in orange. Use it on paper or black, never on blue. On the website it never goes above 50px tall.

Color story

The colors of the cutting bench: a black blank, a white sleeve, the blue pen that numbers every jacket, the hot orange curl coming off the stylus, and the kraft mailer it all ships in.

Sleeve white 60Blank black 22Ballpoint 105
Pairs that work
  • Black on sleeve white: all body text
  • Ballpoint blue on sleeve white: links, buttons, numbers
  • Swarf orange on black: the logo's RC, one accent per piece
  • Black stamp on kraft: packaging
Pairs to avoid
  • Orange on blue, or blue on orange: they vibrate and neither reads
  • Orange for small text on sleeve white: too low in contrast
  • More than one orange element per page, post or label
  • Kraft anywhere but packaging and booth

Voice and tone

The voice stays the same everywhere: the person who'll hand the band their records. The tone shifts with the moment.

Words

Say

  • "Cut," "lathe-cut," "cut one at a time"
  • "Records," "your run," "your release"
  • Exact prices and dates
  • What lathe-cut sounds like, honestly: a bit quieter than a pressed record, with its own character
  • Athens, by name

Don't say

  • "Pressing" or "pressed." We don't press records.
  • "Vinyl" as the main word. Records are cut on polycarbonate or PVC blanks; say "records."
  • "Audiophile," "studio-grade," or anything that oversells the sound
  • Our costs, margins or what partners are paid
  • Exclamation points in more than one line per message
