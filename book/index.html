Deposit and refunds

  • A 50% deposit books your date and pays for materials. The balance is due at pickup, or before we ship.
  • Nothing is cut or printed until you approve the proof.
  • Your deposit is refunded in full until we order materials or start on your audio. After that it isn't refunded.
  • If we miss your promised date by 7 days or more, you can cancel for a full refund.
  • Records damaged in shipping (photo within 7 days) or with our mistakes are remade free.
