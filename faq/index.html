Questions, answered

What bands ask before their first run: how lathe-cut records sound, what to send us, and what happens after you book.

The records

What is a lathe-cut record?

A record cut one at a time on a lathe. A diamond stylus carves your music into a blank disc in real time, so a 10-minute side takes 10 minutes to cut. Every copy is made by hand in Athens.

How is it different from pressed vinyl?

Pressed records are stamped from molds, which only pays off at hundreds of copies and usually means a long wait. Lathe cutting has no big minimum and no plant queue, so you can make 10 or 20 records for a release show.

The trade-off is sound: a lathe cut usually has a little more surface noise than a pressed record. Most bands and fans treat that as part of the charm.

Will it play on a normal turntable?

Yes. Our records play at standard speeds on standard turntables.

What sizes do you make?
  • 7": up to about 6 minutes a side. Singles and short EPs.
  • 10": up to about 12 minutes a side. EPs and most band releases.
  • 12": coming soon, for full albums.

Louder, bass-heavy music may need a little less time per side to cut cleanly. We'll tell you if yours does.

What's the smallest run?

10 records. Most bands start with our Athens Starter: twenty 10" records, fully finished. See Prices.

Can I get colored or clear records?

It depends on what blanks we have in stock. Ask when you book and we'll tell you what's possible.

The sound

Do you master for vinyl?

Yes, and it's included. Mike prepares each side for the lathe: setting the level, keeping the bass centered, taming harsh "s" sounds, and sequencing the side so it cuts clean and plays right.

Mono or stereo?

Ask us when you book. We'll tell you what we can cut for your record.

Why might my record sound different from my streaming master?

Vinyl has physical limits. Very loud, heavily limited masters and deep stereo bass have to be adjusted so the needle stays in the groove. Songs near the end of a side also lose a little detail, because the groove moves more slowly near the center. We'll put quieter songs last if you let us.

Can you do a compilation of several bands?

Yes. Each artist's tracks are mastered on their own so they sit well together, which takes a little longer and adds a small per-artist fee.

Your audio

What files should I send?
  • WAV or AIFF, 16 or 24-bit, 44.1 to 96 kHz.
  • No MP3s, and don't convert an MP3 to WAV.
  • Either each song on its own, with the order for Side A and Side B, or one finished file per side with about 2 seconds of silence between songs.
Should I send a mastered or unmastered mix?

Send the best version you have at its original resolution. If you have one, a dynamic master without heavy limiting gives us more room to make it sound great on vinyl.

Do I need permission for the music?

Yes. You need to own the music and artwork you send, or have permission to use them. Covers need a mechanical license, which is quick and inexpensive to get.

Artwork

Can you design our labels and jacket?

Yes, design is included. Send photos, logos and ideas, and Josh designs printed labels for both sides and the jacket.

We have our own artwork. What sizes?

We'll send exact templates with your quote. To get started:

  • 300 DPI PNG or print-ready PDF.
  • Separate files for the Side A label, Side B label, and the jacket front and back.
  • Keep text and logos away from the edges and the center hole.

Booking

How long does a run take?

We give you a firm date with your quote, counted from when we have your final audio and artwork. Need it faster? Rush is available when the schedule allows.

How do I pay?

A 50% deposit books your date and pays for your materials. The balance is due when you pick up, or before we ship. We send a Square invoice for each payment, so you can pay by card or bank transfer. At pickup we also take cards in person.

Do we see everything before you cut?

Yes. We send a proof of your labels and jacket, and nothing is cut or printed until you approve it. Changes after you approve are billed at our cost.

Can we cancel?

Yes. Your deposit is refunded in full until we order your materials or start on your audio. After that, the deposit covers what we've already spent, so it isn't refunded.

If we miss your promised date by 7 days or more, you can cancel and get your deposit back in full.

What if our audio or artwork is late?

Your date is counted from when we have final files. If they arrive more than 30 days after you book, we'll set a new date together. If we don't hear from you for 90 days, the deposit is kept and the booking closes.

Pickup or shipping?

Pickup in Athens is free. We can ship anywhere in the US at cost.

What if a record arrives damaged or has a mistake?

Send us a photo of the box and the record within 7 days and we'll make a new one free. If we made a mistake on a record, we remake it free too.

Can we order more later?

Yes. Ask us about reordering the same records.

Ready to make a record?

Tell us what you're making and we'll send a quote.
